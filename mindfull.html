About this project

A Mindfull is a personal passion project, an extraction shooter I am building from scratch in Godot Engine 4. The world is full of lore: an ancient conflict between the spirit realm and the physical world, with Sarroth as the central antagonist trying to permanently banish all spirits.

You play as a carrier, a person who can host spirits. You lost your wife to a bunch of monsters which The God of Gods sent, because she secretly carried more than one spirit, which is forbidden. Each spirit floats visibly behind your character and grants you its abilities in combat.

The project is actively in development. I release playable test builds to my Discord community for feedback on game feel, balance, and bugs. Community input directly shapes which features I prioritise each sprint.

Building A Mindfull teaches me everything from game architecture and state machines to shader effects and audio management, skills that complement my software development studies.

Key highlights

  • Spirits float visually behind the player character
  • Every spirit has a unique attack, passive, and active ability
  • Deep lore: world-building with factions, history, and named NPCs
  • Entirely solo, art, code, design, and audio all by me
  • Active Discord community with regular test builds
  • State-machine architecture for smooth player and enemy behaviour

Game Features

What makes A Mindfull unique

Spirit Collection

Encounter spirits scattered throughout the world. Earn their trust through quests or combat challenges. Each spirit you recruit appears floating behind your character in real time.

Unique Ability System

Every spirit has three abilities: a standard attack, a passive effect that's always active, and an active skill with a cooldown. Combining spirits creates powerful synergies you discover through experimentation.

Combat System

A combination of vampire survivors and hades, targeting a slow, but engaging combar system. You can find or craft your weapons and defeat enemies to gather shards, which are the currency of the game.

Lore & World building

Lead a vengeful crusade against the God of Gods, ally with the dark Sarroth, or save the spirit world, or plunge into procedurally generated extraction runs to loot shards and escape with your life.

Health & Status Bars

HUD displays player health, spirit energy, enemy health bars, and active ability cooldowns. Visual feedback is a priority, every hit, heal, and status effect is clearly communicated.

Community Testing

Regular test builds are released to the Discord community. Testers report bugs, vote on features, and take part in balance discussions, making development a collaborative effort.

Tech Stack

Tools and technologies behind A Mindfull

Godot Engine 4

Game Engine

Open-source game engine for 2D and 3D games. Handles rendering, physics, audio, input, and scene management out of the box.

GDScript

Scripting Language

Python-like language native to Godot. Used for all game logic: player movement, combat, AI, UI, and ability systems.

Pixel Art

Art & Assets

Custom pixel art for all characters, sprites, environments, and UI elements, drawn and animated by hand to match the game's tone.

State Machines

Architecture Pattern

Player and enemy behaviour is driven by finite state machines (Idle, Walk, Attack, Hurt, Dead). Makes adding new behaviour predictable and bug-free.

Version Control

Challenges & Learnings

Challenges I faced

  • Designing an ability system flexible enough to accommodate wildly different spirit powers without rewriting core logic.
  • Making enemy AI feel natural, enemies need to follow the player smoothly while not overlapping each other or the camera.
  • Balancing the game solo without a dedicated QA team, early test builds were often over- or underpowered.
  • Managing project scope as a solo developer, saying no to feature creep to keep momentum.

What I learned

  • Game architecture: scene trees, signals, autoloads, and resource files, Godot's paradigm for structuring large projects.
  • State machine patterns that translate directly to software engineering, I now use similar patterns in Java projects.
  • Community management: how to gather, filter, and act on feedback without losing your creative vision.
  • Shipping discipline, regular releases, however small, keep motivation high and community engaged.
  • Pixel art fundamentals: colour palettes, animation principles, and how art style affects perceived game quality.
