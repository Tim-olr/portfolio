About this project

Very Mindful is the biggest game I have built so far. You pick a character and a spirit in the Spirit Realm, a white city on an island above the clouds, and drop into one large procedural world. There you fight, mine the ground into blocks, build with them, loot chests and take on the Sovereign that guards each biome.

To keep what you found you have to extract: hold an uplink beacon through waves of enemies. Die and everything you carried is lost. Money and materials you bring home buy permanent upgrades, new characters and alternate abilities.

I made the characters, spirits, enemies, Sovereigns and buildings myself in Blender and brought them into Godot, where they are animated and given their abilities.

Key highlights

  • 23 playable characters, each with a signature weapon and unlockable abilities
  • 100 spirits with their own attack, passive, active and animations
  • A 4 km procedural world with seven biomes, caves and structures
  • Mine the ground into blocks and place them to build
  • Sovereigns with multi phase boss fights and mini bosses on deeper levels
  • Every character, spirit and Sovereign modelled in Blender

Features

What you can do in the game

Characters

Every character has a primary attack, an alt fire, a utility and a movement ability, plus a passive. Alternate abilities and locked characters are bought with money and materials.

Spirits

A spirit fights next to you with its own attack, passive and active. Each one is unlocked by an achievement and has a role: Attack, Support, Evader or All Round.

Procedural World

Seven biomes with cliffs, ravines, caves with ore veins, buildings with loot, buried stashes and totems. The world streams in chunks around the player with a day and night cycle.

Breaking and Building

Mine the ground into blocks with its own mining action, and break trees, rocks and crates with your attacks for materials. Place blocks anywhere in a run to build cover, bridges or walls, or build in your own rooms that you buy in the Room Hall.

Sovereigns and Bosses

Each biome has a Sovereign waiting at its Seat. Defeat it for its Core and bring all seven home. Deeper levels have their own mini bosses with signature skills.

Extraction

Hold an uplink beacon through three phases and two enemy surges to bank your loot. The second surge brings a champion.

Run Modifiers

Artifacts, cards, bonds, world events and eight tiers of difficulty change every run. Patrons give contract chains with permanent rewards.

Tech Stack

The technologies powering Very Mindful

Godot 4

Game engine

Rendering, physics, scenes and UI. Every menu is its own scene with signals connected in the editor.

GD Script

Gameplay

All game logic: characters, spirits, enemies, Sovereigns, the director that spawns enemies and every run system.

Blender

Models

All characters, spirits, enemies, Sovereigns and buildings are modelled and painted in Blender.

GLSL Shaders

Rendering

A shared clay shader with vertex animation for walking creatures, procedural leaves, rocks and an outline pass.

Worker Threads

Performance

Terrain chunks are built on background threads with four levels of detail, and pooled projectiles keep combat fast.

Challenges & Learnings

Challenges I faced

  • Streaming a 4 km world without stutters: chunk meshes on worker threads, levels of detail and collision only near the player.
  • Making models built from code look like a real game instead of toys.
  • Keeping 100 spirits, 23 characters and dozens of artifacts working together without breaking each other.
  • Balancing runs so that dying hurts but extracting always feels earned.

What I learned

  • Procedural generation: noise based terrain, biome regions, caves and structure placement.
  • Writing shaders and procedural animation with springs instead of keyframes.
  • Modelling in Blender and getting the models into Godot.
  • Structuring a large codebase with many small, clearly named methods so it stays readable.
